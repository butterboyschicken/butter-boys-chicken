Good chicken behaviour

Your dinner. Three simple steps.

STEP 01

Marinade

Mix the 18 g marinade blend with 240 ml water. Coat the chicken thighs, cover and refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight.

Marinade blend — 18 g

  • Sriracha powder — 1 tbsp / 11 g
  • Salt — 1 tsp / 3.7 g
  • Pepper — 1 tsp / 2.6 g

You add: 1 cup water / 240 ml

STEP 02

Dry dredge

For the coating, combine the 138 g dry mix with 2 cups flour. Add 1 tbsp of the dry mix to the marinated chicken thighs and shake, then toss the chicken in the flour-and-dry-mix coating.

Dry mix — 138 g

  • Potato starch — ½ cup / 83 g
  • MSG — 1 tsp / 3.45 g
  • Salt — 1 tbsp / 14.5 g
  • Baking powder — 1 tsp / 3.25 g
  • Cayenne — 1 tsp / 2.2 g
  • White pepper — 1 tsp / 2.6 g
  • Black pepper — 1 tsp / 2.6 g
  • Onion powder — 1½ tbsp / 11.5 g
  • Garlic powder — 1½ tbsp / 11.5 g

You add: 2 cups flour / 285 g

STEP 03

Wet dredge & fry

Mix the 45 g wet dredge blend with ¾ cup water. Dip each piece of coated chicken in the wet dredge, then toss again in the dry mix until rough and craggy.

Wet dredge — 45 g

  • Buttermilk powder — ¼ cup / 32 g
  • Salt — 1 tsp / 3.7 g
  • Cornflour — 1 tbsp / 8.3 g

You add: ¾ cup water / 180 ml

Fry

Fry at 160°C for 6–12 minutes, depending on piece size and equipment. Do not rely on time alone: check the thickest piece with a food thermometer and ensure the centre reaches 75°C for 30 seconds before serving.

Keep chicken refrigerated while marinating. Use separate clean utensils and surfaces for raw chicken and ready-to-eat food. Cooking time varies with the size of the thighs and the fryer; always verify the chicken is thoroughly cooked.
